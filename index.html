Endotoxin check

Checks your injection against the FDA/USP <85> endotoxin ceiling using the vial, water and dose above.

Vial EU is the total for the whole vial from an independent LAL test, not EU/mg and not the vendor's CoA.
Thresholds & how this works

Compares a dose, a stack or a whole cycle against the FDA/USP <85> endotoxin reference ceiling, the way PeptideSafe does. Maximum EU per dose for IV, IM and SubQ is 5 EU/kg; intrathecal and spinal routes use 0.2 EU/kg. Standard U-100 pens deliver 1 unit = 0.01 mL; check your pen's dial.

Research and education only. Informational estimates, not dosing instructions or a safety guarantee. The FDA/USP limit is a manufacturing reference, not a statement that any dose is safe for any person. Modeled on PeptideSafe.

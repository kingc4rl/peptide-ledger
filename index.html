Endotoxin check

Compares a dose, a stack or a whole cycle against the FDA/USP <85> endotoxin reference ceiling, the way PeptideSafe does. It uses the vial, water and dose from the calculator above. Enter the total EU for the whole vial from an independent LAL test (not EU/mg, and not the vendor's CoA).

Check your pen's dial markings. Standard U-100 pens: 1 unit = 0.01 mL.
FDA endotoxin thresholds by body weight

Maximum EU per dose for IV, IM and SubQ at 5 EU/kg. Intrathecal and spinal routes use 0.2 EU/kg.

Research and education only. Informational estimates, not dosing instructions or a safety guarantee. The FDA/USP limit is a manufacturing reference, not a statement that any dose is safe for any person. Modeled on PeptideSafe.
